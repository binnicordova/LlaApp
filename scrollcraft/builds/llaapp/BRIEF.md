# BRIEF — llaapp.com

Mixed provenance. Topics 3, 6, 7 and the peak are **verbatim user decisions**
(selected from offered options). Topics 1, 2, 5 are **authored** from positioning
already established with the user in this project and are labelled as such.
Not a self-authored brief: creative authority was exercised by the user on the
four decisions that govern the page.

## 1. Vibe, three to five words — AUTHORED
Cold, exact, unhurried, Pacific.

References (authored, non-website, per the interview rule):
- Bathymetric and admiralty charts of the Strait of Georgia. Information as beauty.
- The first eight minutes of *Blade Runner 2049*: vast, quiet, no hurry, one figure.
- A Teenage Engineering manual: nothing decorative survives, and it still has warmth.

Explicitly not: AI-purple, neon, glass cards, claymation, "futuristic".

## 2. Scroll journey, in sequence — AUTHORED from established positioning
Above the coast → down through the weather → under the surface → the work they
actually shipped → back up to a horizon and one action.

## 3. Energy curve — USER
Premium-minimal throughout. Quiet start, quiet close, one loud act in the middle.

## 4. Feeling curve, and the peak — USER chose the peak; curve authored

| Act | Feeling | What on screen causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Above | Stillness, slight vertigo | A cold contour field seen from far above, moving slowly under the reader's own hand |
| 2 Coast | Orientation, recognition | The lines resolve into a coastline the reader half-recognises as home |
| 3 Below | Quiet, held breath | The surface passes. Everything goes dark and structural. **Authored silence: no copy for ~0.4 of this leg.** |
| 4 The proof wall | **PEAK.** Surprise, then weight | Out of the dark, lit panes surface one after another, each one real product footage from work that actually shipped |
| 5 Horizon | Resolution, then trust | The panes sink, one horizon line holds, and the page resolves on the founder: named, pictured, personally accountable |

**The peak, as a visitor would say it to a colleague:**
"They didn't tell me they built the NFL app. They showed it to me, running, at the bottom of the ocean."
Lives in act 4, which owns the largest scroll span on the page by a clear margin.

**Authored silence:** act 3 carries no copy through its first 40%. That is
deliberate, so the verification pass does not read it as dead scroll.

## 4b. Attribution (added 2026-09-17, on the founder's correction)

The client products are work **Binni Cordova helped build as an engineer**, not
engagements delivered by LLAAPP.com. The page, the schema and both llms files
say so in those words. The earlier wording ("software this team shipped") was an

rather than asserted, because it has not been supplied.

## 4c. Re-point (2026-09-17, third revision)

The founder confirmed the page narrative did not carry the business objective:
"LLAAPP.com. Custom software & AI transformation. We consult & build elite
custom AI app products engineered for millions of downloads. Own the 2027 AI
transform." An audit of the labels and the five locales found the spine was a
local compliance consultancy (Vancouver / The audit / Your data / The work /
Talk) selling to a different buyer.

The spine now traces the objective: **Vancouver / Consult / Build / Scale / 2027**.
Data residency survives as a capability clause inside Build rather than as the
thesis of an act. "Millions" is carried at the peak as evidence (apps that
reached millions of users) rather than as an assertion. 2027 is substantiated by
the sourced Vancouver sovereign-compute build-out, not used as bare urgency.

Voice is first person singular throughout. The objective's "we" was not adopted,
because the founder asked to be personally attending clients and a solo practice
writing "we" is the overclaim corrected in 4b.

## 5. One thing no site they have seen does — AUTHORED (seeded by the user's peak choice)
Every agency page in this market *claims* Coca-Cola and the NFL as text in a
logo strip. This page plays the actual software, recorded from the real shipped
apps, surfacing out of a dark ocean. The proof is not a claim, and no competitor
can copy it, because they do not have the footage.

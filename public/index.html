AI Transformation Questions We Solve

Common questions from business owners and entrepreneurs in Vancouver, across Canada and the USA, and in Europe evaluating AI adoption.

What does an AI transformation consultant actually do?

We audit your operations to find the highest-ROI AI use cases, then design and build the AI agents, LLM integrations, or automations that deliver them.

How do I know if my business is ready for AI?

If you have repeatable manual workflows, unstructured data, or predictable customer questions, you are ready. We confirm readiness with a fixed-scope audit first.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT or Copilot?

Off-the-shelf tools help one person at a time. We integrate AI into your systems and data, with security and governance, so the whole business benefits.

Do you build custom AI agents, or only advise on strategy?

Both. We deliver the strategy and audit, and our senior engineering team builds and ships the AI agents, RAG systems, and integrations.

How much does an AI transformation engagement cost?

Engagements typically start with a fixed-scope AI Readiness Audit, followed by phased implementation priced by scope. You get a clear estimate before build starts.

Can you add AI to our existing software instead of rebuilding?

Yes. Most engagements add AI capabilities to systems you already run, rather than replacing them, which minimizes risk and time to value.

Do you work with small businesses, or only large enterprises?

Both. Our engineering discipline was built delivering mission-critical systems for global enterprises, and we apply that same rigor to growing businesses.

Which regions do you serve for AI consulting?

Vancouver and Canada, the United States including San Francisco, Texas, and Chicago, and Europe including Germany, Italy, and Spain, remote-first everywhere.

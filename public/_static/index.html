AI Transformation Questions We Solve

Common questions from business owners and entrepreneurs in Vancouver, across Canada and the USA, and in Europe evaluating AI adoption.

What does an AI transformation consultant actually do?

We audit your operations to find the highest-ROI AI use cases, then design and build the AI agents, LLM integrations, or automations that deliver them. We hand off systems your team can run and extend, with documentation and training so results continue after our engagement ends.

How do I know if my business is ready for AI?

You are ready if you have repeatable manual workflows, unstructured data such as documents, tickets, or emails, or customer-facing processes with predictable questions. We validate readiness with a fixed-scope AI Readiness Audit before recommending any build work, so you invest only where the ROI is clear.

How is this different from just using ChatGPT or Copilot?

Off-the-shelf AI tools like ChatGPT or Copilot help one person at a time. An AI transformation partner integrates AI into your actual systems and data, with security, governance, and reliability built in, so the whole business benefits from AI, not just individual users experimenting on their own.

Do you build custom AI agents, or only advise on strategy?

Both. We deliver strategy and AI readiness audits, and our senior engineering team builds and ships the AI agents, RAG systems, and integrations we recommend. It is the same team that has built mission-critical software for global enterprises, so strategy and execution stay fully connected end to end.

How much does an AI transformation engagement cost?

Engagements typically start with a fixed-scope AI Readiness Audit, priced flat, followed by phased implementation priced by scope, integrations, and data complexity. You receive a clear, written estimate before any build work begins, so there are no surprise costs once your AI transformation project is underway.

Can you add AI to our existing software instead of rebuilding?

Yes. Most engagements add AI capabilities, agents, copilots, and automation into the systems you already run, rather than replacing them outright. This minimizes risk, preserves your existing workflows and data, and shortens time to value compared with a full rebuild of your current software stack.

Do you work with small businesses, or only large enterprises?

Both. Our engineering discipline was built delivering mission-critical systems for global enterprises like Coca-Cola, the NFL, and major banks, and we apply that same rigor to AI transformation engagements for growing businesses and entrepreneurs, scaling the scope and cost to match your team's size and budget.

Which regions do you serve for AI consulting?

We serve Vancouver and Canada nationwide, the United States including San Francisco, Texas, and Chicago, and Europe including Germany, Italy, and Spain, with remote-first delivery for businesses anywhere in these regions. Local market knowledge is paired with senior engineers available across matching time zones for every client.
